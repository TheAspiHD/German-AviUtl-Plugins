# How do I use this?

**This is for AviUtl 1.10! DO NOT USE FOR DIFFERENT VERSIONS**

Exedit.auf and exedit.auo contain the translations.
You have to replace the files with the ones found in here.
If you have a later version of AviUtl (1.14 and earlier), here is how to hack the files to your liking and replace the strings with your translation:
Open said files in ResourceHacker and check the DIALOG and MENU folders. Replace the strings in "" with your translation.
Then compile the script with the green play button before saving the file.

Boom, you have your translation.
